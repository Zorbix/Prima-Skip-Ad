Prima Skip Ad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy describes the Prima Skip Ad browser extension, version 1.1.0.

Information processed on your device

The extension stores only your selected video skip interval (a number of seconds) in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not use Chrome Sync for this setting. When you click the skip button, the extension accesses the active tab and checks video elements, their source availability, display state, playback position and duration to move playback forward. This information is processed temporarily on your device and is not saved as browsing history.

No collection or transmission to the developer

The extension does not transmit personal information, page contents, video sources or browsing activity to the developer or third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, external network requests or remotely hosted executable code. It does not sell or share user data.

Permissions

activeTab temporarily allows access to the current page after you invoke the extension. scripting runs the video-seeking function on that page when you press the skip button. storage remembers the interval locally between uses.

Retention and deletion

The interval remains in local extension storage until it is replaced or the extension is removed. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings. No developer-held copy exists.

Scope and changes

This policy covers the extension. Websites you visit and the platform hosting this policy have their own data practices. If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the corresponding release.

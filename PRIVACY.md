# Prímový Skip Ad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026


This policy describes the Prímový Skip Ad browser extension, version 1.2.0.


## Information processed on your device

The extension stores only your selected video skip interval (a number of seconds) in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not use Chrome Sync for this setting. When you click the skip button, the extension accesses the active tab and checks video elements, their source availability, display state, playback position and duration to move playback forward. If you enable automatic advert skipping on the TV app, it also reads programme and advert timestamps from the page's player state, the seekable video ranges and the forward button state. It checks these locally while the mode is enabled, including after the popup closes. The mode and attempted breaks are kept only in that page's memory until disabled or the page is reloaded. This information is not saved as browsing history.


## No collection or transmission to the developer

The extension does not transmit personal information, page contents, video sources or browsing activity to the developer or third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, external network requests or remotely hosted executable code. It does not sell or share user data.


## Permissions

activeTab temporarily allows access to the current page after you invoke the extension. scripting runs manual video seeking and the optional automatic mode on that page. The automatic mode runs in the page's main JavaScript context to read its player timestamps. storage remembers the interval locally between uses.


## Retention and deletion

The interval remains in local extension storage until it is replaced or the extension is removed. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings. No developer-held copy exists.


## Scope and changes

This policy covers the extension. Websites you visit and the platform hosting this policy have their own data practices. If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the corresponding release.
